Apple Calendar

Show events from your iPhone's Calendar app here. They stay read-only: change them in Apple Calendar.

How to get the link
  1. Open the Calendar app on your iPhone and tap Calendars at the bottom.
  2. Tap the (i) next to an iCloud calendar.
  3. Turn on Public Calendar, tap Share Link… and then Copy.
  4. Paste it below. Anyone who has this link can see that calendar, so keep it to yourself.

Siri and places

Siri: say "Hey Siri, add to My day" and then anything: "buy bread tomorrow 9", "take out the trash when I get home", or a note.

How to set it up (2 minutes)
  1. Open the Shortcuts app, tap +.
  2. Add Dictate Text.
  3. Add URL Encode (it uses the Dictated Text).
  4. Add Text: paste the Siri link, then right after it insert the URL Encoded Text variable.
  5. Add Get Contents of URL (it uses the Text).
  6. Add Speak Text or Show Result so Siri tells you what it saved.
  7. Name the shortcut Add to My day.

Place reminders: "When I get home…" reminders pop up when your iPhone sees you arrive. Copy a link for each place you use:

How to set it up
  1. Shortcuts app > Automation > + > Arrive (or Leave).
  2. Choose the place (your home address, school…), pick Run Immediately.
  3. Add Get Contents of URL and paste the link for that place.
  4. That's it: the app sends you a notification with what to remember there.
