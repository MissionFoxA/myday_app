How to get the link
  1. Open the Calendar app on your iPhone and tap Calendars at the bottom.
  2. Tap the (i) next to an iCloud calendar.
  3. Turn on Public Calendar, tap Share Link… and then Copy.
  4. Paste it below. Anyone who has this link can see that calendar, so keep it to yourself.
